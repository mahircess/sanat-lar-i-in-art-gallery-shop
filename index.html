Maya Chen

San Francisco, California · Joined 2021

Maya Chen is a contemporary painter whose work explores the boundaries between abstraction and representation. Drawing inspiration from the natural landscapes of the Pacific coast, her paintings capture ephemeral moments of light and atmosphere. Her work has been featured in galleries across North America and Europe.
